01 / Our story

It starts with
a little curiosity.

The stars are too cool to feel out of reach. We love the way a simple glance at the night sky can turn into a hundred questions: what keeps a planet in orbit? How do stars pull on one another? What happens when they get too close?

Astrophysics can get complicated quickly. We made lumi to offer an easier way in: a place to see ideas in motion, try things for yourself, and make sense of a little more of the universe. You don’t need a physics background. Just bring your curiosity.

Our mission

See it. Try it.
Learn a little.

We want to make learning about space feel approachable. With simple controls and a universe you can experiment with, lumi turns abstract ideas into something you can watch and explore. Drop in for a minute or follow a question a little further. Leave knowing something new.

02 / Our orbit

The things we
come back to.

✧

Education through exploration.

Learning starts with a question. We connect what you see with the physics behind it, so each orbit and interaction becomes a chance to understand a little more.

◌

A universe for everyone.

You shouldn’t need to know the equations before you can enjoy the ideas. We value clear explanations, approachable controls, and room to learn at your own pace.

✳

Something worth sharing.

Space is fascinating, and we want more people to get that same spark of excitement. lumi is our way of sharing something we love with anyone who wants to look closer.
